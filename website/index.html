How it works

From setup to screening in three simple steps

Everything is handled by Maro, not added to your plate.

  1. 01

    Collect consent from parents, digitally

    Maro sends plain-English consent forms to every family, tracks who has responded, and makes sure no student is screened without the right permissions in place.

  2. 02

    Screen students with validated surveys

    Students complete validated screeners (like the PHQ-9 for depression) online using any school device. No paper, no manual tracking, no coordination headaches.

  3. 03

    Analyze results and connect students to care

    Instantly see which students need follow-up, pull school and district-level reports, and refer students to care. Parent communication is built in the whole way through.

For families

Some parents will have questions. Here's how Maro prepares you.

Consent

“Did I agree to this?”

Every family receives a plain-English consent form before screening begins. Maro tracks every response, timestamps every submission, and gives your school a full audit trail.

Privacy

“Who sees my child's data?”

Student data is encrypted and stored securely, with strict permissions so it's only viewed in-platform by authorized school personnel. Maro is fully SOPPA and COPPA compliant.

Opting out

“Can I say no?”

Absolutely. Parents can opt their child out at any time through a digital consent form. Maro makes opting out as easy as opting in.

Follow-up

“What happens if my child screens at-risk?”

No student is flagged without a follow-up plan. Maro documents every referral, helps schools notify parents through the platform, and connects families to support.

Referrals

Easily refer at-risk students to the support they need

Maro makes it easy to find the right support for every child, and handles the parent communication too.

  • Search in-network and virtual providers near each student
  • Send a pre-written referral email to parents in one click
  • Document every next step for your records

What is Maro?

Maro is a mental health screening platform built for K-12 schools and districts. We make it easy to run universal screenings, identify students who may need support, and connect families to care, all in one place.

How long does implementation take?

Most schools are up and running within a few weeks. Our team handles the setup, training, and onboarding so your staff doesn't have to figure it out alone.

How does parent consent work?

Maro handles it digitally. We send consent forms to families automatically and track responses in real time. No paper, no chasing down signatures. Parents can opt in or out at any time.

Is student data secure and FERPA compliant?

Yes. Maro is FERPA and COPPA compliant. All student data is encrypted, securely stored, and never shared outside of authorized school personnel and parents.
